What GTA CLIPPER is

GTA CLIPPER is a personal tool run by the owner of the @GTAVIMasterClips accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It records public Twitch livestreams that it has permission to clip, cuts short highlight clips, adds captions and titles, and posts them to those accounts only. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign up for or use it.

Using it

  • Only the owner signs in to GTA CLIPPER, and only with their own Google, Facebook and TikTok accounts.
  • By signing in, the owner allows it to post clips to their own accounts and read the basic account details needed to do that. It does nothing else with the accounts.
  • The owner can remove its access at any time in each platform's account settings.
