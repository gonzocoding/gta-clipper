GTA CLIPPER – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

GTA CLIPPER is a personal tool run by the owner of the @GTAVIMasterClips accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It records public Twitch livestreams that it has permission to clip, cuts short highlight clips, and posts them to those accounts only.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: with the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), it uploads videos to @GTAVIMasterClips, sets the visibility of the videos it uploads, and reads that channel's basic details to make sure it is uploading to the right channel. It does not read, change or delete any other YouTube data.
  • Instagram (through Facebook): with the owner's permission (Facebook Login), it posts Reels to the owner's Instagram account @gtavimasterclips through the owner's linked Facebook Page, and reads that account's username and posting limit. It does not read or change anything else.
  • TikTok: with the owner's permission (TikTok Login), it sends clips to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts, which the owner reviews and posts in the TikTok app. It reads only the basic account information TikTok requires for this.
  • Twitch chat: it reads public chat messages from the livestream it is clipping, to detect exciting moments and write clip titles. Messages are kept in memory for about 10 minutes and are not stored or shared.

What it stores

  • Sign-in tokens for the owner's YouTube, Instagram/Facebook and TikTok accounts, stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • The video clips it makes, stored on the owner's computer until they are deleted.

It does not collect, sell or share personal information about anyone, and it is not offered to other users.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time: for YouTube at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, for Instagram/Facebook in Facebook Settings > Business integrations, and for TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
